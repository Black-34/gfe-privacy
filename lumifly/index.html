Privacy Policy – Lumi Fly

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Lumi Fly ("the Game") handles information. The Game is developed by Hüseyin Dumlu ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information ourselves.

Game progress, stars, purchased items, settings and your best score are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • device and app information (e.g. device model, operating system, language),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • interactions with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game or reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (for example star packs, "Remove ads" and the monthly "Lumi Pass" subscription). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never receive your payment details. The store only tells the Game which items you own, so they can be unlocked. Subscriptions renew automatically each month and can be cancelled at any time in your Google Play or App Store account.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

3a. Leaderboards (Google Play Games / Game Center)

If you use the online leaderboards, you are signed in with Google Play Games (Android) or Apple Game Center (iOS). Your scores and your public player name from that service are sent to Google or Apple and shown to other players in the leaderboard. We do not receive or store this information ourselves. This is governed by Google's or Apple's privacy policies.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. In the optional "Kids" level no full-screen ads are shown, and all ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the third-party services described above, we do not share any information.

6. Security and retention

We do not store your personal data on our own servers. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests regarding advertising data should be directed to Google. You can still contact us at any time.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
